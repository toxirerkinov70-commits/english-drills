Every Day vs Right Now Toʼgʼri 0 Qolgan 20

Present Simple · Present Continuous — 20 ta mashq

Soʼzlarni tartibga sol: every day yoki right now?

Har bir mashqda soʼzlar aralashib ketgan. Soʼzni bosing — u yuqoridagi qatorga tushadi; qatordagi soʼzni bossangiz, u orqaga qaytadi. Gap toʼliq tuzilgach Tekshirish tugmasini bosing: javob shu zahoti toʼgʼri yoki xato ekani koʼrinadi. Xato boʼlsa, tartib qaysi soʼzdan buzilgani belgilanadi, izoh va toʼgʼri variant chiqadi — keyin qayta urinib koʼrishingiz mumkin.

Mashqlar birma-bir ochiladi: keyingi gap joriy gapni toʼgʼri yechganingizdan keyin chiqadi. Qaysi gapda qaysi zamon kerakligini oʼzingiz aniqlaysiz — gapdagi vaqt ifodasiga qarang. Sahifa oxirida bonus bor: uni 20 ta mashqdan kamida 18 tasini birinchi urinishdayoq toʼgʼri yechgan oʼquvchi ochadi.

Bonus qulfda

0/20 · kerak: 18

Bonus 20 ta mashqdan kamida 18 tasi birinchi urinishda toʼgʼri yechilganda ochiladi. Ichida nima borligi hozircha sir — avval mashqlarni tugating.

Present Simple

Har doim takrorlanadigan ish, odat, jadval va umumiy haqiqat uchun.

  • Tasdiq
    I / You / We / They + work
    He / She / It + works
  • Inkor
    donʼt / doesnʼt + work
  • Savol
    Do / Does … work?
  • Belgilar
    every day, usually, always, often, sometimes, never, on Mondays
  • Misol
    I study English every day.
    He goes to work by bus.

Present Continuous

Aynan hozir yoki shu kunlarda vaqtincha davom etayotgan ish uchun.

  • Tasdiq
    am / is / are + working
  • Inkor
    am not / isnʼt / arenʼt + working
  • Savol
    Am / Is / Are … working?
  • Belgilar
    now, right now, at the moment, today, tonight, this week, Look! Listen!
  • Misol
    Iʼm learning English now.
    She isnʼt having breakfast today.
Uchta tez eslatma

1. Farqi bitta savolda. «Har doim shundaymi?» → Simple. «Aynan hozir sodir boʼlyaptimi?» → Continuous. Shuning uchun bir gapda ikkalasi ham boʼlishi mumkin: I usually work at home, but now Iʼm working at the cafe.

2. -ing yozilishi. run → running (oxirgi undosh ikkilanadi), make → making (oxirgi -e tushadi), study → studying (-y saqlanadi).

3. Continuousʼda ishlatilmaydigan feʼllar. know, like, love, want, need, understand, have (egalik maʼnosida) — ular faqat Simpleʼda keladi: I know him, Iʼm knowing him emas.

Natija shu qurilma xotirasida saqlanadi — sahifani yopsangiz ham yoʼqolmaydi.
